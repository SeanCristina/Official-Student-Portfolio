Photography

A selection of my best captured images, shot with my Sony α6400. Ever since I saved up enough money to buy this as my first camera at 12 years old, I have never stopped learning about this tool and understanding to use its functionality and versatility help me translate a specific artistic to a tangible snapshot. I'm especially drawn to capturing the tranquility of nature and the way a single frame can freeze a memorable experience in time to be relived countless more times. These images each have seen a long personalized editing and grading process in Adobe Lightroom, a software which I have also utilized to enhance the voice of my shots.
