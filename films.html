Filmmaking

Short Films

My path in producing my own videos and short films has been everything but linear, but it's a process I have steadily honed for the past half-decade. Every new video I make, my true artistic intent is showcased even more accurately, which is one of the most exciting and motivating parts of this journey because I feel a sense of improvment of my skills in this craft. I'm motivated to continue doing so because it's my way of displaying the hard work and dedication I'd put into a single project if it's worth it.

Instead of learning this skill in an organized course, I've spent over six years taking the artistic elements I love about a certain visual or auditory piece and implement it in my own unique way. I enjoy working on these projects independently because the resulting projects feel much more organic and individually stylized, as opposed to formulaic and cliché. A lot of these videos are really short because each second of runtime represents the best work I could produce at the time. Each film or video is a highly-inspired distinctive experiment in storytelling through image and sound.

Check out the rest of my video production journey on "The Channelists" on YouTube, containing all of the fun projects my brother and I have created together over the years! (youtube.com/TheChannelists)
