Capturing moments through a lens, telling stories through film,
and building things my own way.

Hi! My name is Sean Cristina. I'm a high school student with a multifaceted passion for photography, short filmmaking, and working closely with the latest technology. I always continue making progress refining my skillset in subjects I'm most passionate about, remaining sharp and familiarized with every emerging innovation in its accompanying fields of study.

I employ a diverse selection of equipment to aid in every step in the process of bringing my creative visualizations to reality through photography and cinema alike, from anamorphic lenses to shape light in such a way to give a dreamy feeling to a scene, to high-performance computing solutions I use to transform a sequence of shots into an artwork, intentionally blending light, sound, color and music together to create an immersive and captivating experience that's truly my own. I greatly enjoy engaging in every part in the creative pipeline, from crafting the initial storyboard with my brother to the intensive visual effects and color editing, to the mastering of the finalized film. I use the same engaged and involved work-ethic in everything I have a genuine interest for.

I also enjoy building, modding and repairing computers, and find creative ways to get my devices to do what I them to do. This portfolio showcases the work I'm most proud of, and documents all my specific projects and endeavors during high school.
