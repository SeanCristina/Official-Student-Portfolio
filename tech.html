02
Modifying

iPod
Mods

When I see an old device, I think of potential. This is especially true if it was originally designed with easy repairability and user-servicibility in mind. I tapped into a specific market in 2024 involving obsolete iPods and discovered many in-depth modification projects that were possible for these little music players. I decided to try these modifications on my own iPods. I researched and bought a high-end soldering iron to learn how to solder the small bluetooth modules and wiring to the internal battery. Not only did these mods heavily modernize the functionality of the iPod, but they also increased the durability of the device and thus, its longevity.

Beyond adding internal bluetooth connnectivity, I've performed storage upgrades from HDD to solid-state, I've replaced degraded batteries, resoldered headphone jack connections, and installed Rockbox on several devices to achieve gapless playback, equalizer support, and FLAC compatibility.

These projects taught me microsoldering, the patience required for working with ribbon cables and PCB solder pads, and how to navigate electrical issues.
